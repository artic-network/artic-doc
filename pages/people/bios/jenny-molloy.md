biography: Dr. Jenny Molloy is an Assistant Research Professor at the University of Cambridge and a Group Leader at the International Centre for Genetic Engineering and Biotechnology (ICGEB, Trieste, Italy), where she develops open technologies for engineering biology. Her research focuses on the potential for local, distributed biomanufacturing of enzymes to enhance access to diagnostics and build capacity for biological research in the global South. Dr. Molloy co-founded four social enterprises and communities, collaborating with partners in Africa, Latin America, and other regions to promote an open, sustainable, and equitable global bioeconomy, including the Open Science Hardware Foundation and Beneficial Bio.
