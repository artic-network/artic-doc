biography: Caroline Colijn is based at Simon Fraser University, where she holds a Canada 150 Research Chair in Mathematics for Evolution, Infection and Public Health. Her work sits at the interface of mathematics and the epidemiology and evolution of pathogens. Her group develops mathematical tools that connect sequence data to the ecology and evolution of infections, including new methods for analysing and comparing phylogenetic trees. She is particularly interested in how co-infection, competition and selection among interacting pathogens drive the development of antimicrobial resistance.
