biography: Prince Akil-Bandali is a molecular biologist at the Institut National de Recherche Biomédicale (INRB) in Kinshasa, Democratic Republic of the Congo, where he has worked since 2021 in the Pathogen Genomics Laboratory. His work focuses on laboratory diagnosis and genomic surveillance of high-priority pathogens, including Ebola virus, mpox virus, SARS-CoV-2, and HIV. He is experienced in DNA/RNA extraction, PCR and RT-PCR, next-generation sequencing library preparation, and result validation under strict biosafety standards. He has contributed to outbreak investigations and peer-reviewed publications and has actively supported capacity building, serving as a trainer for genomic surveillance and laboratory implementation in the DRC, Central African Republic and Cameroon
